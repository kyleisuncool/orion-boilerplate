# Open questions

**The register of decisions nobody has made yet.** Numbered, never renumbered — a closed question keeps its number. Items arrive here from `orion-lint` (a finding carried two cycles) and `orion-cascade` (a warning carried two runs). Each is written as a forced binary:

`N. **Question** — Option A / Option B. Raised YYYY-MM-DD.`

Close one by appending `**CLOSED YYYY-MM-DD** — the answer`.
