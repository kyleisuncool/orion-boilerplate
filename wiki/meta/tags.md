# Tag taxonomy

## Area tags

One tag per Area, carried by its hub and everything under it — its Projects and its `knowledge/` notes. Registered here by `orion-instantiate`.

| Tag | Area |
|---|---|
| *(none yet)* | |

**Retired Area tags.** When an Area archives or dissolves, move its row here. Pages keep the tag as provenance; `orion-lint` stops treating it as a live Area.

| Tag | Area | Retired |
|---|---|---|
| *(none)* | | |

**An Area tag records where something entered the vault.** Add a second one if a page genuinely belongs to two Areas; never strip the original.

## Topic tags

For `3. Resources/Research/` — reusable knowledge that belongs to no single Area. **Topic tags only there, never an Area tag.** Add to this list rather than inventing ad hoc tags.
